Verification Centre Guide

Verification builds client trust and unlocks full platform access. Verified providers receive a badge that increases conversion rates.

Verification steps
1
Identity Verification
Upload a valid government-issued ID (passport or national ID card). We verify it within 24–48 hours.
2
Professional Credentials
Upload any relevant licences, certifications, or professional registrations. Required for regulated services (legal, financial, medical).
3
Verified Badge
Once approved, your profile displays a Verified badge. Verified providers rank higher and earn more client trust.
✅ Verified
Pro tips
📄
Submit clear documents. Blurry or incomplete uploads cause rejections and delays — use well-lit, high-resolution scans.
⏱️
Verify early. Verification can take up to 48h — complete it before you start listing services to avoid delays.
